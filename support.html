Common questions

A reminder did not arrive. Check that notifications are allowed for Fullgrid in iPhone Settings, that the bell on the Today screen is not on (it pauses all reminders) and that the time is not inside your quiet hours. With "Only when behind" on, a reminder is skipped when you have already logged enough by that time, and a habit that has reached its goal is not reminded again until the next day, week or month.

Reminders stopped after a while. Reminders are scheduled up to two weeks ahead and are extended every time you open the app or use a button on a notification. If neither happens for that long, a last notification asks you to open Fullgrid. Opening it once brings them back.

The important reminder made no sound. It is a Time Sensitive notification with a longer sound, not an alarm. It is silent when the iPhone is muted, and a Focus lets it through only if Time Sensitive notifications are allowed for that Focus.

Where are the widgets? Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets need iOS 17 or later. Open the app once, then add a widget and search for Fullgrid. Home Screen tiles log a step with a tap; the Lock Screen ring shows progress.

How do I move my data to a new phone? Settings > Backup > Export backup saves a file wherever you choose. On the new phone, Import backup replaces the data there with the contents of that file.

Where is my data? Only on your device. Fullgrid has no account and never connects to the internet. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I change the language? Fullgrid follows the iPhone's language. To choose one just for Fullgrid: iPhone Settings > Fullgrid > Language.

常见问题

提醒没有来。请检查：iPhone「设置」里是否允许满格发送通知；今天页的铃铛是否开着（开着表示全部提醒暂停）；时间是否落在免打扰时段内。打开「仅在进度落后时提醒」后，到点时如果已经记够，这一条会跳过；目标达成后，本周期内不再提醒。

提醒过一阵就停了。提醒最多提前安排两周，每次打开 App 或点通知上的按钮都会顺延。如果这么久两样都没做，最后会有一条通知请你打开满格；打开一次就恢复。

重要提醒没有声音。它是带较长提示音的「时效性通知」，不是闹钟。iPhone 静音时不会出声；专注模式下，只有允许了时效性通知才能穿透。

小组件在哪里？主屏幕和锁定屏幕的小组件需要 iOS 17 或更高版本。先打开一次 App，再添加小组件并搜索「满格」。主屏幕的色块点一下就记一次；锁定屏幕的圆环显示进度。

怎么把数据搬到新手机？「设置 › 备份 › 导出备份」把文件保存到你选的位置；在新手机上「导入备份」，那里的数据会被这份文件替换。

数据存在哪里？只在你的设备上。满格没有账号，也从不联网。详见隐私政策。

怎么换语言？满格跟随 iPhone 的语言。要单独为满格选语言：iPhone「设置 › 满格 › 语言」。
