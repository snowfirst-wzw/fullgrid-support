Privacy Policy · English

Fullgrid does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising, and the app never connects to the internet.

Your habits, entries, notes and settings are stored only on this device. The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read the same local data. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Notifications are used only to deliver the reminders you set. Photo access is add-only and is used when you save a share image. A backup file you pick is read only to import it.

A backup file is created only when you ask for one, and it goes wherever you choose to save or share it. If your device backup includes app data, it is handled by your device maker under your own account.

Deleting a habit also deletes its history. Deleting the app removes all of its data from this device.
